Design

Precision, from every angle.

The A9 e-tron was drawn with a single rule: every surface must do aerodynamic work. What remains is the quietest shape we have ever built.

01

Aerodynamics as ornament

Flush door handles, a sealed underbody, and virtual exterior mirrors shape the airflow. The result is a drag coefficient of 0.22 — and silence at 130 km/h.

02

Light, engineered

Digital Matrix LED headlights project guidance onto the road ahead. The rear light bar — one continuous line of red — is the only color the car wears.

03

An interior of restraint

One curved display. Four physical controls that matter. Materials chosen for how they age, not how they photograph: recycled microfiber, open-pore wood, aluminum.

04

800 volts, 18 minutes

The 800-volt architecture charges at up to 320 kW. Ten to eighty percent in 18 minutes — roughly the time it takes to drink a coffee properly.
