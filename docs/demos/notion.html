Q4 Product Sprint

Last edited by Mara Chen · Oct 2, 2026

💡
Scope freeze Friday. No new tickets after standup — anything later goes to the Q1 backlog.

This page tracks the final product sprint of the year. The goal is simple: ship the new onboarding flow and cut trial-to-paid time by 15% before the holiday freeze.

Sprint plan

▾ Week 1–2 · Build

Engineers pair on the onboarding rewrite. Design ships the empty states and the progress indicator. Nothing merges without a passing checklist review.

▾ Week 3 · Measure

Run the funnel analysis, watch ten session replays, and interview five trial users who dropped off at step two. Bring one surprise finding to Friday's review.

▾ Week 4 · Polish & ship

Fix the top three friction points, write the changelog, and record the two-minute walkthrough video. Ship on Thursday; Friday is buffer, not a workday.
