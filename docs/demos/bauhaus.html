01exhibitions

geometry of the everyday

one hundred objects reduced to their essentials. a chair is a square that holds weight. a lamp is a circle that gives light. the exhibition traces how primary forms solved real problems — no decoration, only decisions.

every piece on display was made by students in their first year. the rule was simple: build it from circles, squares and triangles, or do not build it at all.

14 — 28 oct · hall a · free entry

honest material

steel is shown as steel. wood is shown as wood. nothing is painted to imitate something it is not. this exhibition collects workshop pieces where the material makes the argument and the finish gets out of the way.

bauhaus trained metalworkers, weavers and potters in the same building as painters. material honesty was not a style choice — it was the curriculum.

02 — 16 nov · hall b · free entry

stage and movement

costumes built from geometry, bodies moving through constructed space. the stage workshop treated the human figure as a shape among shapes — precise, mechanical, and strangely joyful.
