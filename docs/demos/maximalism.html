THE MANIFESTO

Read it loud
Rule #1

Minimalism had a good run. Ten years of white space, grey buttons and interfaces that apologize for existing. We got bored.

KHAOS CLUB makes clothes for people who take up space. Clashing prints. Colors that argue with each other and win. Type so big it needs its own postcode. If your outfit doesn't start a conversation, it's furniture.

We print in small runs, pay our printers properly, and ship every order with a sticker pack — because a laptop without stickers is just a sad rectangle.

"Dress like the fireworks, not the audience."
