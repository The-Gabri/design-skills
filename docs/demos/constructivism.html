From the manifesto

The poster is not decoration.
The poster is an order!

01 The diagonal

Level lines are for ledgers. Our compositions ride one sharp diagonal — type, bars and wedges all charge the same way. Movement is meaning.

02 Three inks

Red for energy, black for structure, paper for the ground. Flat, violent, legible. Any fourth color must earn its place or leave.

03 The shout

Short orders, not descriptions. The exclamation mark is a structural element. If the wall doesn't answer back, start over.

— Workers' Art Committee, October 1925 · reprinted 2026

The method

Cut. Paste.
Agitate!

Klutsis and Rodchenko never painted a poster when they could build one. A cropped photograph, a red disc, a bar of type on the diagonal — fact plus force on a single plane.

The festival archive holds 200 reproductions: Lengiz book posters, LEF magazine covers, cinema placards. Every one of them shouts. None of them whispers.
