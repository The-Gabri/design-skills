the idea

the plan was to build a voice recorder. but voice recorders are boring, so we built a tape machine that fits in your pocket instead. it records everything, all the time, onto a spinning reel that isn't really there. quite cool actually.

spin the reel back to hear what you just missed. press the orange encoder to make it sound worse, on purpose. that's the whole manual — well, almost.
