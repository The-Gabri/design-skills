Why it earns its spot
on the fence post.

Always-on e-ink

Glanceable in full sun, readable at night with a soft backlight. The display shows what changed since you last looked — not a dashboard you have to decode.

Hyperlocal nowcasts

“Rain in 12 minutes” — not for the metro area, for your block. Nimbus fuses its own sensors with regional radar to call the next hour, minute by minute.

60-day battery

The e-ink screen draws power only when it changes. Between reads the station sleeps, waking each second to sample and each minute to think.

Private by design

Forecasts are computed on the device. Your location never leaves your home network unless you ask the app to share it — and you can revoke that anytime.
