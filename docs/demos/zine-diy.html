Manifesto of the copy room · pt. 7

We do not have a layout program. We have scissors, a glue stick that is mostly air, and twenty minutes on the library copier before the librarian notices. This is not a limitation. This is the entire point.

A glossy magazine needs your attention. A zine needs your hands. Cut this page out. Circle the show you are going to. Write your phone number in the margin and hand it to a stranger. If the toner smudges, that is the design working.

We print 200 copies because the tray holds 200 sheets. We charge fifty cents or a trade because nobody here is getting rich and the glue stick still costs money. When the copies run out, the issue is over. Scarcity is not a marketing strategy. It is a paper tray.
