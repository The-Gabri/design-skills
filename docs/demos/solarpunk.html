Good questions

The grove does. Every member holds an equal share, decisions are made at monthly meetings, and if you move, your share transfers to the next household. Nobody here rents the sun from a landlord.

Yes — most groves reserve shares for renters. You can hold a farm bed, draw from the shared battery, and vote at meetings. Some groves even have a rooftop agreement template your landlord can sign.

A typical first share is about 240, paid once, and it covers your part of one project. Groves also run repair cafés and skill swaps so nobody pays full price for labor. Grants have covered the first share for 1 in 5 members.

The grid is still there as a backup — solarpunk isn't a bunker fantasy. The battery smooths days; the shared plan smooths weeks. Groves publish their real numbers, cloudy weeks included.
