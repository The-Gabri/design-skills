L'Objet

The Flacon

Blown in Lorraine, stoppered in lilac glass. Choose a tint — the scent within never changes.

Sauge

vitrum salviae — sage glass

Sage glass, the colour of the lilac leaf at dawn — our signature tint since 1897.

Every flacon is mouth-blown, so no two carry quite the same breath of air in the glass. The lilac signet is pressed into the stopper while it is still warm.

Noted in the atelier ledger — three vials, one of each family, will be wrapped in tissue and twine. We write to confirm the posting day.

"Distilled by hand, adorned by nature — our iris rests twelve weeks in oak before it ever meets the flacon."
Hélène Lilas · Maître Parfumeur

The Atelier

14 Rue des Lilas, Paris XIe. The copper stills run Tuesday to Saturday; on Saturdays we decant the week's pressing while the metal is still warm, and visitors may watch.

Hours

Tuesday – Saturday, ten until six. Closed Sunday and Monday, when the flowers rest and so do we. Appointments for private consultations at the marble counter.

Correspondence

Write to atelier@maison-lilas.example — every letter is answered by hand within the week, sealed with lilac wax. No machines reply here.
