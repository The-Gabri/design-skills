The manifesto

1918: The programme

Manifesto I, published in De Stijl, vol. II, no. 1, November 1918. Excerpts — the words that set the grid in motion.

I

De Stijl Manifesto

November 1918 · Leiden

1

There is an old and a new consciousness of time. The old is connected with the individual. The new is connected with the universal.

2

The war is destroying the old world with its contents: individual domination in every state.

3

The new art has brought to the fore what the new consciousness of time contains: a balance between the universal and the individual.
