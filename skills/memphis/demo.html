The night, in three acts

Every corner of Club Totem gets the Memphis treatment: laminate surfaces, clashing pastels, and geometry that refuses to behave. Switch tabs — the chaos is organized, we promise.

The Bacterio Room

Walls wrapped in black squiggle laminate, straight off a 1981 Sottsass print. The bar serves neon spritzes in triangle glasses. Photography is mandatory; subtlety is not.

9 PM – 11 PM

Terrazzo Floor

The main dance floor: a thousand colored chips under your feet, Italo disco overhead, and a ceiling mobile of spinning triangles. Bring shoes you can lose in.

11 PM – 2 AM

The Totem Finale

A seven-meter totem of stacked shapes drops confetti at 2 AM. Everyone gets a sticker that says "I clashed on purpose." Nobody leaves beige.

2 AM – late

DJ Carlton

Named after the most famous bookcase in postmodern history. Opens the night with synth-funk and a strict no-ballads policy.

House rules

Posted at the door, printed on hot-pink laminate, in 96-point type. Ignorance of the rules is no excuse — they are very loud.

  • Dress code: louder than the carpet. Beige will be confiscated.
  • Compliment at least one stranger's clashing outfit.
  • Triangles, circles, and squiggles are all valid dance partners.
  • The totem gets the last dance. Do not argue with the totem.
  • Photography encouraged — tag every squiggle.
  • If your shoes match your shirt, you are underdressed.
  • Asymmetry is the grid. Stand at an angle.
  • Minimalism is a rumor. Leave it at the door.
