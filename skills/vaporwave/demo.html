Since 1987

THE LABEL

Midnight Mall Records started in the back of a shuttered record store, between the neon sign repair shop and the future. We never left.

REWOUND BY HAND

Every cassette is spooled, degaussed, and rewound by an actual human who still owns a pencil for that exact purpose. Tape hiss is not a bug. It is the room tone of 1987.

CHROME POSITION ONLY

Type II or nothing. We press vinyl at 45 RPM, ship cassettes in chrome, and burn CD-ROMs that include one (1) unskippable secret track. Collectors understand.

THE MALL NEVER CLOSED

It just changed frequency. Our releases are the soundtrack of the food court at midnight — part elegy, part invitation. Come back to the atrium. The fountain is still running.
