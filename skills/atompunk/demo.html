Three pavilions of pure wonder

SEE THE EXHIBITS!

Every hall is air-conditioned by science and staffed by smiling guides in chrome helmets. Pick a pavilion to peek inside.

Touch a Real Nose Cone!

Recovered from a genuine suborbital hop, our Mercury-style capsule sits open for climbing inside (children under twelve must be accompanied by an adult with a camera). Wind-tunnel demos every hour, on the hour, with complimentary earplugs.

The Friendly Atom!

Our 40-foot glowing atom model splits — harmlessly, we promise — six times daily. Learn how uranium keeps your toast warm, powers your vacuum cleaner, and will absolutely, positively never be used for anything upsetting. Brought to you by the Consolidated Cheerful Atom Company.

Live Like It's 1975!

Tour the push-button kitchen where dinner cooks itself in 30 seconds flat. Marvel at the picture-phone, the robot vacuum named "Dusty," and a television so large it has its own zip code. Housewives faint daily at 2 p.m. — be there!

Do not miss

THRILLS! CHILLS! ROCKET FUEL!

Three can't-miss attractions, each scientifically proven to widen eyes.

RIDE

Ride the Space Highway!

Rocket-buses leave the North Terminal every 20 minutes for a thrilling 40-second trip to "orbit" (a parking lot with excellent stars painted on it). Seatbelts are chrome.

SHOW

The Radar Dome Revue!

A rotating stage, a 200-piece theremin orchestra, and our famous sweeping radar that finds absolutely nothing — because the skies are friendly! Matinees daily.

DINE

Dine at the Atomic Diner!

Boomerang tables, starburst napkins, and a milkshake so thick it requires a uranium straw. Try the "Fallout Float" — it glows, but only a little, and only at night.
