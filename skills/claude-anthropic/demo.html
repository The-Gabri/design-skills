Built for the way writing actually happens

Writing is thinking, slowed down. Fieldnote is designed to match that pace — patient, precise, and honest about what it doesn't know.

01

Reads like an editor, not a search box

Fieldnote holds your whole draft in mind — tone, structure, the argument you were building — and responds to what you meant, not just what you typed.

02

Corrects you, kindly

When it disagrees, it says so plainly and shows its reasoning. It won't always get the tone right — when it doesn't, tell it; it learns faster from corrections than from praise.

03

Your words stay yours

Drafts are never used to train models. Export anytime, delete everything with one click, and read the plain-language privacy policy that explains exactly what leaves your device.
