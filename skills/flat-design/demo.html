Features

Everything a budget should do, nothing it shouldn't.

No spreadsheets, no guilt trips. Just a clear picture of your money and a gentle push in the right direction.

Auto-categorized spending

Every purchase lands in the right pocket by itself. Rent, groceries, nights out — sorted before you've put the phone away.

Nudges, not nagging

A friendly heads-up when a pocket runs low — two days before it matters, not two hours after it's too late.

Real-time sync

Bank, card and cash in one place, updated the second money moves. Your balance is always the truth, never a guess.

Saving goals that stick

Name it, set a date, watch the ring fill. Pockets moves a little aside every payday so you don't have to think about it.

Bank-grade privacy

Read-only bank connections, 256-bit encryption, and your data is never sold. Delete everything with one tap, anytime.

Shared pockets

Split rent, trips and groceries with a partner or flatmates. Everyone sees the shared pockets — nobody sees your private ones.
